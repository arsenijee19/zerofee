# Unit Economics

ZeroFee revenue is fixed SaaS subscription revenue, not creator GMV.

Seed plans:
- Starter: `$19/mo`
- Creator: `$49/mo`
- Pro: `$99/mo`
- Business: `$199/mo`

Major variable costs to track:
- support/moderation;
- email/broadcast volume;
- storage and bandwidth;
- API/webhook usage;
- failed payment recovery;
- Stripe platform billing fees;
- compliance reviews.

Recommended telemetry:
- SaaS MRR;
- active creators;
- active members;
- creator GMV;
- support tickets per creator;
- API/webhook/email usage by plan;
- gross margin by plan.
